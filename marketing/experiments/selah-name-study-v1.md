# Selah name fit test · v1

Status: prepared; not yet published or promoted through an external account.

## Why test names

Search results show several unrelated Bible products using “Selah,” including `selah.so`, `theselah.app`, and `myselahh.com`. The label is not uniquely identifying this app in search. “QuietWord” is also already used by Bible apps, so it is excluded from this shortlist. This is an initial search screen only, not domain, trademark, or app-store clearance.

## Five naming approaches

| Variant | Approach | Korean display name | English display name | Market hypothesis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| a | Category/search clarity | 성경 묵상 | Bible Meditation | Makes the app type immediately clear; may blend into generic search results. |
| b | Small habit promise | 한 장, 한 순간 | One Chapter, One Moment | Makes the first reading action feel approachable and concrete. |
| c | Warm companionship | 말씀곁 | Word Beside You | Frames the reading moment as a quiet companion. |
| d | Action sequence | 읽고, 머물고, 기록하기 | Read. Pause. Reflect. | Names the app's actual read-and-reflect flow. |
| e | Reflection outcome | 마음에 남은 말씀 | What Stays With You | Centers what a reader may want to keep from a passage. |

The landing page adapts each approach for `ko`, `en`, `fil`, `ja`, `es`, `pt-BR`, `zh-CN`, and `zh-TW`. These are working translations, not native-speaker approvals. The product remains Selah during the test; the feature list, artwork, description, layout, and call to action stay constant so the name concept is the main variable.

## Shareable variant URLs

The published URL will be `https://delight0517.github.io/selah-bible-meditation/name-study.html`. Add `?lang=<locale>&variant=<a-e>` to pin a candidate for a matching promotional creative. Omit `variant` to assign one candidate randomly and keep it stable in that browser. The page is `noindex`: use these URLs for campaign traffic, not as competing Google landing pages. Append allowlisted `utm_source` and `utm_medium` values when sharing from a measured channel.

Example Korean campaign copy for every candidate (change only the candidate name in the creative): “**{후보 이름}** — 성경 한 장을 천천히 읽고, 마음에 남은 말씀과 묵상을 기록해 보세요. 이 이름이 마음에 들면 같은 Selah 앱을 열어 직접 살펴보세요.” Keep the visual, description, and CTA fixed across the five names. Make separate English and locale-native creatives with the same promise. No campaign has been distributed yet.

## Measurement

- Sticky anonymous browser assignment: one candidate per browser. Cloudflare adds only country and first-level region; the page sends a supported UI locale, coarse web/device class, coarse source/medium labels, experiment name and candidate campaign. No account IDs, IP, raw referrer, Bible text, or reflection text are sent by the page.
- The page records `global-funnel-v1` exposure and CTA events with campaign IDs `selah-name-fit-v1-a` through `selah-name-fit-v1-e`. The app receives the same campaign tag, so existing reader-start, 30-second/2-minute reading, saved-reflection, signup, and return events can be compared by country, locale, and name campaign in the detailed experiment summary.
- A click means interest in opening the same Selah app under that candidate label. It is not proof of declared brand preference, a real person, or an activated user. Search impressions, social impressions, page exposures, clicks, readers, reflection saves, signups, and weekly active users remain separate.
- Do not shortlist a market winner below 50 exposures per name per market, and do not choose on clicks alone; inspect reading and saved-reflection evidence too. Fifty is a floor for observing, not a significance claim. Check a name with local readers and do trademark/domain/app-store clearance before any real rebrand.
- Internal QA visits marked `selah_qa=1` are excluded from experiment events. No user-level personal data is collected for self-exclusion.

## Current market evidence and release gate

On 2026-10-05, the connected Search Console property `https://delight0517.github.io/` returned zero Selah rows for both page+country and query+page; the report was settled only through 2026-09-29, before the recent localized homepage indexing window. Therefore there is no evidence yet for a country winner or name winner. The name-test page is `noindex` to avoid thin-page SEO and to prevent a name-test interstitial from replacing the real app search result. It is ready for a tracked share/campaign URL once a non-private, verified promotion channel is available. No post, paid promotion, or product rebrand has been made.
