A quiet way to begin

What to write in a Bible journal

You do not need a perfect format or a long entry. After reading a chapter, notice one thing, ask one honest question, and write a response you want to remember.

Three gentle prompts

  1. Notice: What detail, person, or repeated idea stood out to you?
  2. Ask: What would you like to understand better or read again?
  3. Respond: What thought, next step, or prayer would you like to carry with you?

One sentence is enough. If nothing comes to mind yet, write the question and leave room to return to it.

A simple example

After reading Matthew 1, an entry might begin:

“I noticed Matthew begins by naming a family line. I want to read the chapter again and ask why this opening matters.”

This is a starting point, not a required interpretation. Stay with what you notice in the passage, and use a trusted study resource or community when you have questions.
