SELAH · BIBLE MEDITATION

A quiet place to read Scripture and write what stays with you.

Selah is a free Bible meditation and prayer journal for a slower reading rhythm. Start with Matthew, take one chapter at a time, and save a short reflection in your own words.

Read, reflect, and return

  • Read Matthew chapter by chapter, with Korean, English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese interface options.
  • Open complete 66-book Bible readers in Tagalog, Spanish, and Brazilian Portuguese.
  • Keep a private reflection and a small response for the day.
  • Return to saved notes and review what you want to remember with quizzes.
  • Share a link that opens the same passage for a friend; your private notes stay private.
  • Use it in a browser on a phone, tablet, or computer. A free BlueCloud account syncs your notes across devices.

A gentle way to begin

Choose a chapter of Matthew, read without rushing, then write one thing you noticed and one response you may want to carry into the day. Selah offers a place to reflect; it does not replace your own reading or community.

The current reading library focuses on Matthew. Bible text availability depends on the selected translation. Your notes are stored on your device unless you sign in and enable account sync.

Questions

Can I use Selah without an account?

Yes. You can read and keep notes on the device you are using. Sign in to BlueCloud when you want account sync across devices.

Can I share a passage without sharing my notes?

Yes. The passage link opens the selected Matthew chapter and does not include your private reflections or prayers.

Does Selah include the whole Bible?

The main meditation workspace starts with Matthew. Separate full-Bible readers are available in Tagalog, Spanish, and Brazilian Portuguese. Each reader names its edition and license.
