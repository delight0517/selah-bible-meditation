# Selah: 100 multi-market campaign models

**Status: research and copy prototypes only. None of these models has been published or paid for.** They are hypotheses to test, not evidence of 100 validated needs. Market describes the campaign's geography; locale and explicit search intent shape the message. Do not infer an individual's faith, denomination, vulnerability, or identity from location or browsing.

## Evidence and boundaries

- Major Bible-app competitors advertise reading plans, audio, offline reading, highlights, bookmarks, notes, translation comparison, dark mode, and sharing. This establishes familiar category patterns, not demand in Selah's markets: [YouVersion feature overview](https://www.youversion.com/bible-app) and [Bible Gateway mobile app](https://search.biblegateway.com/app/).
- The Philippines has national readership/language and mobile-access signals; Brazil has limited survey signals from broader reading and a selected Bible-engagement sample. Neither establishes Selah-specific demand. See the dated citations and sample caveats in [GROWTH_RESEARCH.md](../GROWTH_RESEARCH.md).
- Korea, Japan, Taiwan, Mexico, Spain, Argentina, and the two US language cohorts remain exploratory absent relevant settled Search Console and downstream product evidence. A localized route or interface language alone does not prove a local audience.
- Existing locale routes are reused; each campaign changes one substantial, relevant first-screen module. Do not create 100 near-duplicate indexable URLs or run 100 simultaneous variants. Country, language, edition, CTA, image, page promise, and campaign tag must match.
- Before release, verify live flow, exact Bible edition and rights. Current product flows suitable for validation include passage/book selection, focused reading, timed meditation, personal reflection/prayer notes, local bookmarks/highlights/share, and manually user-created review cards. Notes do not sync across devices unless the signed-in sync flow is used. The GPT writing guide requires BlueCloud sign-in and is text guidance, not GPT voice mode. Audio, published reading plans, original-language study, automatic Bible quizzes, and side-by-side bilingual reading are not ready-to-promise features. Together-reading requires a separate runtime acceptance check.
- Measurement sequence: landing exposure → CTA → reader start → focused reading at 30s/120s → reflection saved → signup → later return. These are separate event stages, not interchangeable user counts. Keep the current 28-day hold and 50 exposures per variant as an early observation floor, not a claim of statistical certainty. Exclude QA. Paid spend remains zero until route attribution and downstream readback work; any test stays within the user's previously authorized ₩20,000 ceiling. The personal Instagram is excluded.

## How to run the portfolio

1. Reconcile the existing generated-mobile-source owner and analytics migration/CI blockers before deploying route attribution. The current PR #215 is undeployed, so production cannot reliably connect a campaign route to reader and reflection outcomes.
2. Validate one end-to-end journey per “Now” feature and locale first; mark failed claims as feature work, not ad copy. Keep one page per genuine intent group with a campaign identifier rather than mass-producing doorway pages.
3. From the 100 drafts, select at most a few independent hypotheses at a time across markets whose edition, translation, channel and measurement are ready. Rotate the remaining concepts after a data review; do not start 100 cells that would fragment already-small traffic.
4. Prefer verified, consent-friendly organic channels first. No connected public channel or publication has been verified by this document. Keep personal Instagram excluded. No ad buy or spend has happened.
5. Record impressions, clicks, external landings, focused readers, saved reflections, signups and activated users separately. If minimum sample or time is not met, keep the variant unchanged and record “inconclusive.” Change a page early only for a broken journey, rights issue, privacy problem, or clear promise mismatch.
